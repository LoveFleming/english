# How Much / How Many 多少？

## 為什麼要學 How much / How many？

買東西想知道價錢？想知道教室有幾個人？想知道要加多少糖？

這些問題都要用到 **How much** 或 **How many**！

它們的意思都是「多少」，但用法不一樣。學會了，你就能問各種「多少」的問題！

---

## Pattern 1：How many + 複數名詞 + are there + 地方？

> 問「有多少個可以數的東西？」

### 句型公式

> **How many + 複數名詞 + are there + 地方？**

回答用 **There is/are...**

### 例句

| 問句 | 回答 |
|------|------|
| How many cooks are there in the kitchen? | There are three cooks in the kitchen. |
| How many students are there in the classroom? | There are thirty students in the classroom. |
| How many dogs are there in the park? | There are five dogs in the park. |
| How many books are there on the desk? | There are ten books on the desk. |
| How many chairs are there in the room? | There are twelve chairs in the room. |

### 重點提醒

- **How many** 後面一定要接**複數名詞**（加 s）！
- 回答用 **There are**（因為是複數）
- 如果只有一個，回答改用 **There is**

| ✅ 正確 | ❌ 錯誤 |
|---------|---------|
| How many students are there? | How many student are there? |
| How many books are there? | How many book are there? |

> **口訣：** How many 後面一定要加 s，因為你問的是「多少個」，當然是複數！

---

## Pattern 2：How many + 複數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？

> 問「可以做幾個／多少個」

### 句型公式

> **How many + 複數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？**

### 常見助動詞

| 助動詞 | 用法 | 例子 |
|--------|------|------|
| **can** | 能力、可以 | How many pancakes can you eat? |
| **do** | 習慣、事實 | How many cookies do you want? |
| **will** | 未來、意願 | How many games will you play? |
| **should** | 建議、應該 | How many questions should we answer? |

### 例句

| 問句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| How many pancakes can you eat? | 你能吃幾個鬆餅？ |
| How many cookies do you want? | 你想要幾塊餅乾？ |
| How many books can she carry? | 她能搬幾本書？ |
| How many eggs do we need? | 我們需要幾顆蛋？ |
| How many songs will they sing? | 他們要唱幾首歌？ |

### 主詞是第三人稱單數時

當主詞是 **he / she / it** 時，助動詞要用 **does**：

| 主詞 | 助動詞 | 例子 |
|------|--------|------|
| you | do | How many cookies do you want? |
| he | does | How many cookies does he want? |
| she | does | How many apples does she need? |
| they | do | How many apples do they need? |

> **注意：** does 後面的動詞要用**原形**！不要加 s！
> ❌ How many apples does she needs? → ✅ How many apples does she **need**?

## Pattern 3：How much + 不可數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？

> 問「多少量」

### 句型公式

> **How much + 不可數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？**

### 什麼是不可數名詞？

不可數名詞就是**沒辦法一個一個數**的東西：

| 不可數名詞 | 中文 | ❌ 不能說 |
|------------|------|-----------|
| water | 水 | one water, two waters |
| milk | 牛奶 | one milk, two milks |
| sugar | 糖 | one sugar, two sugars |
| rice | 米飯 | one rice, two rices |
| money | 錢 | one money, two moneys |
| time | 時間 | one time, two times（times 當「次數」可以） |
| homework | 功課 | one homework, two homeworks |
| bread | 麵包 | one bread, two breads |

### 例句

| 問句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| How much sugar do you need? | 你需要多少糖？ |
| How much water do you drink every day? | 你每天喝多少水？ |
| How much milk does the baby want? | 寶寶想要多少牛奶？ |
| How much money do you have? | 你有多少錢？ |
| How much homework do you have today? | 你今天有多少功課？ |
| How much rice is there in the bowl? | 碗裡有多少飯？ |

### How many vs How much 對照

這是考試最愛考的重點！

| | How many | How much |
|--|----------|----------|
| 搭配 | **可數**名詞（加 s） | **不可數**名詞 |
| 問的是 | 多少**個** | 多少**量** |
| 例子 | How many apples? | How much water? |
| 例子 | How many students? | How much sugar? |
| 例子 | How many eggs? | How much milk? |

### 容易錯的地方 ⚠️

| ❌ 錯誤 | ✅ 正確 | 原因 |
|---------|---------|------|
| How many water do you drink? | How **much** water do you drink? | water 不可數 |
| How much apples are there? | How **many** apples are there? | apples 可數 |
| How many money do you have? | How **much** money do you have? | money 不可數 |
| How much students are there? | How **many** students are there? | students 可數 |
| How many rice do you need? | How **much** rice do you need? | rice 不可數 |

> **判斷方法：** 你能說「一個、兩個、三個」嗎？可以的話用 **How many**，不行的話用 **How much**！
> - 一顆蘋果、兩顆蘋果 ✅ → How many apples
> - 一個水、兩個水 ❌ → How much water

## Pattern 4：Which do/does + 主詞 + 原形動詞, A or B?

> 問「你選哪一個？」

### 句型公式

> **Which do/does + 主詞 + 原形動詞, A or B?**

### do vs does 怎麼選？

| 主詞 | 助動詞 | 例子 |
|------|--------|------|
| I | do | Which do I choose, A or B? |
| you | do | Which do you like, tea or coffee? |
| we | do | Which do we buy, this or that? |
| they | do | Which do they prefer, cats or dogs? |
| he | does | Which does he want, pizza or pasta? |
| she | does | Which does she like, tea or coffee? |
| it | does | Which does it prefer, meat or fish? |

> **口訣：** I / you / we / they 用 **do**，he / she / it 用 **does**。跟現在簡單式一樣！

### 例句

| 問句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Which do you play, tennis or badminton? | 你打哪一個，網球還是羽球？ |
| Which do you like, tea or coffee? | 你喜歡哪個，茶還是咖啡？ |
| Which does she want, pizza or pasta? | 她想要哪個，披薩還是義大利麵？ |
| Which do they need, pens or pencils? | 他們需要哪個，原子筆還是鉛筆？ |
| Which does he prefer, dogs or cats? | 他比較喜歡哪個，狗還是貓？ |

### 回答方式

| 回答 | 中文 | 說明 |
|------|------|------|
| I like tea. | 我喜歡茶。 | 選其中一個 |
| I like both! | 兩個我都喜歡！ | **both = 兩個都** |
| Neither. | 都不喜歡。 | 兩個都不選 |

> **both** 是超實用的字！當兩個都想要的時候就用 it！

## 總整理

| 句型 | 用法 | 公式 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **Pattern 1** | 問多少個（在某處） | How many + 複數名詞 + are there + 地方？ | How many students are there in the classroom? |
| **Pattern 2** | 問可以做幾個 | How many + 複數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？ | How many cookies do you want? |
| **Pattern 3** | 問多少量 | How much + 不可數名詞 + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞？ | How much sugar do you need? |
| **Pattern 4** | 問選哪個 | Which do/does + 主詞 + 原形動詞, A or B? | Which do you like, tea or coffee? |

### 超級重點

1. **How many = 可數名詞（加 s）** → How many apple**s**
2. **How much = 不可數名詞** → How much water
3. **do 搭配 I/you/we/they，does 搭配 he/she/it**
4. **助動詞後面的動詞用原形** → does she **need**（不是 needs）
5. **both = 兩個都**
